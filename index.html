Care that comes to you, with respect for your home and your time

I'm Meena, a home nurse. I started this service because many patients, especially elders and people recovering from illness or surgery, find it hard to travel for simple procedures that a nurse can do safely at home.

Every visit follows the same standard: clean hands, sterile single-use supplies, clear explanation of what I'm doing, and respect for your family's routine. I follow your doctor's prescription, and I'll tell you when something needs a doctor's attention.

Common questions

Do you provide home visits?

Yes. Every service on this page is delivered at your home.

Are you available on Sundays?

Please call or message to confirm Sunday and holiday availability. We will do our best to fit you in.

Do you offer emergency service?

For life-threatening emergencies call 108 or go to the nearest hospital. For urgent nursing needs, call us and we will reply as fast as we can.

Do you give injections?

Yes, all types as prescribed by your doctor. Please keep the prescription and medicine ready.

Can you collect blood for lab tests?

Yes. Tell us which tests your doctor advised and we will arrange collection at home.

Do I need a doctor's prescription?

For injections, IV drips and catheter removal, yes. It keeps you safe.

How much does a visit cost?

It depends on the service and distance. We tell you the charge before we come.
